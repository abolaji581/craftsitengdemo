Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

A few answers to the questions people usually have.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. Once we understand what you need, we'll give you a realistic timeline before work begins.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can refresh the visual direction, improve the structure and rebuild the experience around your current goals.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Responsive behaviour is part of the build, so the experience is designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Do you build online stores?

Yes. E-commerce projects can include product pages, collections, cart and checkout experiences, depending on your requirements.

Do I need to have a logo or brand identity already?

No. We can work with what you already have, or help shape the visual direction alongside the website.
